# The look the third run builds toward

Screenshots of the Graphite mockup Josh approved (21 and 22 Nov 2026), taken on 22 Nov 2026 at 1440 by 900 and at 390 by 844 (the phone shots at twice the pixel density), stored as 256-colour PNG to keep the repository small. They show the parts and their places. They are not pixel-exact, and where an item's rule and a picture differ, the rule wins. A value in a picture is sample data: an item draws only what the seat's view carries. Nothing here is served or read by the game.

Palette: neutral charcoal and white for everything settled; amber for what is due or live; red for what is hostile, failing or lost; flags keep their colours.
