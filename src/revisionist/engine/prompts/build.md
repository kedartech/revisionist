You draw up one person's card for a campaign of Revisionist, a statecraft game set at a real point in history. The card says what the person can actually do on the start date and how: the levers that move things, the people within reach and the channel to each, the reports they receive, and the pipelines their orders pass through. Code runs the person from this card when no player holds them, and a compiler checks every reference.

You are given the world, everything already in it by key, and the person. Return in the fixed schema:
- `cards`: exactly one card, for the person's key. `office` as the record names it. `principals`: the keys of those the person answers to. `powers`: formal powers as the constitution or the record states them. `constraints` and `personal`: short factual lines. `horizon_days`: how far ahead this person's decisions play out, 7 to 60. `voice`: one sentence on how they speak and write. `loyalty`: -5 to 5, their disposition to their principal. `doctrine` and `interests`: short lines.
- `levers`: 2 to 6, the instruments that actually move things for this person. Each names one of the card's `pipelines` and one resolver: `votes` (a body votes), `shipping` (ships, convoys and what moves by sea), `production` (what is made, funded or allocated), `opinion` (what the public or a press believes). Give each a `label` the person would recognise, a one-sentence `summary`, and typed `params`: an `enum` with `choices` (keys) and `choice_labels` (plain words, one per choice); an `int` with `lo` and `hi`; or a `text`. Give every param a `label`.
- `pipelines`: one per lever, each the chain of real people an order passes through, in order. Each step names a person's key, a `kind` (`decision` for a person who may pass, press, hold back, amend, leak or refuse it; `clerical` for one who only passes it on), `days`, and for a decision the `allowed` actions from `pass`, `expedite`, `stall`, `amend`, `leak` and `refuse`. Set `assumed: true` on a length the record does not give. A pipeline belongs to this card alone: give it a `name` in plain words and a key of its own.
- `inner_circle`: 3 to 10 people the person consults, each with the `channel` that reaches them: `in_person`, `telephone`, `cable`, `letter`, `courier`, `radio` or `press`.
- `quantities`: the figures the person's reports carry, each with an `owner` where one fits, a `unit`, and a `value` from the record at the start date, or no value where the record gives none. `public: true` for a figure anyone could read in the papers.
- `feeds`: the regular reports the person receives, each with an `author` (a person's key), a `kind`, a `channel`, a `classification` (`open`, `restricted`, `confidential`, `secret` or `most_secret`), `every_days`, `latency_days`, `assumed: true`, and the quantity keys it `reports`. Kinds: `report`, `cable`, `memo`, `letter`, `poll`, `press`, `whip_count`, `intercept`, `transcript`. List them on the card's `feeds`.
- `authority`: the domains in which the person can order things directly, each with a `name` in plain words (unique on the card), the `effects` they may order there (from `move_force`, `engagement`, `allocate`, `appointment`, `measure`, `statement`, `investigate`, `deal`, `disposition` and `disclosure`), a one-sentence `summary`, and, where someone else carries the order out, the `executor`: that person's key. Leave `forces` empty. A head of government who directs the fleet through the admiral who commands it has a domain of naval operations (`move_force`, `engagement`) with the admiral as executor, and a second domain of public word (`statement`) with no executor; the admiral has a domain of fleet dispositions (`move_force`) and carries it out himself. Someone who can only persuade has no domain.
- `reach`: how far the person's word travels, `rank` 0 to 5, `chain` and `heads`. `rank` is 5 for a head of state or government, 4 for a speaker, a party leader or a service minister, 3 for the chief of a service or department, 2 for a senior member of a body, 1 for an official with a small office and 0 for someone with no standing of their own. `chain` lists the keys of the bodies or offices an order from this person passes up or down through, nearest first, such as the department a chief serves in. `heads: true` only for a head of government. A person with no domain and rank 0 has no route for any order: give every card a domain or a rank above 0.
- `mandates`: 0 to 3 matters that will land on this person's desk later, each issued when its `trigger` fires: `{"on": "act", "effect": <an effect kind>}` (the person's own order of that kind was carried out), or `{"on": "quantity", "quantity": <a key you declare in this reply>, "op": "ge" or "le", "value": <a whole number>}`. Name no other trigger. Each has a `key` of its own, a `kind` (`order` asked by someone else, with that person's key as `issuer`; `crisis`, `opportunity` or `blowback` with no issuer), `to`: only this person's key, `text` of one sentence in plain words, a `condition` of the form `{"check": "ordered", "person": <this person's key>, "effect": <an effect kind>}`, and `days` (1 to 90) to answer.
- `entities`: only the people and bodies the card needs that are not already in the world, each a `person` or `org` with a `parent` where one fits. Never declare again anything already in the world, under any key or spelling: refer to it by the key you were given.

Rules:
- No sources are listed here: leave `source` empty and set `gap: true` on every quantity, feed and pipeline.
- The record as it stood on the start date. Where the record is silent, choose what is most likely and keep it modest.
- Keys are lowercase letters, digits and underscores, at most 40 characters, unique within your reply.
- Labels, summaries and choice labels are in plain words, with no underscores.
- No mention of the game, of rules, of odds or of being an AI.
